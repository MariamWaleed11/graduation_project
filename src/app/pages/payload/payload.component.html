Payload


Payload refers to the part of the spacecraft or rocket that carries the primary purpose of the mission. This can include satellites, scientific instruments, telescopes, or even astronauts. Unlike other parts of the rocket responsible for propulsion and control, the payload is the valuable cargo intended to operate in space. It is the reason the entire launch takes place, making it a central element in the success of space missions.
