Telemetry


Telemetry is the process of collecting data from a spacecraft and transmitting it back to Earth for monitoring and analysis. This data can include information about the spacecraft’s position, speed, temperature, battery levels, and the health of various onboard systems. Telemetry allows mission controllers to track the performance of the spacecraft in real-time and respond quickly to any anomalies. Without telemetry, it would be impossible to ensure the success and safety of space missions over long distances.
