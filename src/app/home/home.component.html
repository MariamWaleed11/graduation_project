Telemetry

The Telemetry Subsystem is responsible for monitoring and transmitting critical satellite data. It ensures system health, enables diagnostics, and supports ground-based analysis and control.

Control

The Control Subsystem manages satellite orientation, stability, and positioning. It utilizes sensors, onboard computers, and actuators to ensure precise navigation and mission execution.

Payload

The Payload Subsystem carries instruments for data collection, imaging, or communication. It operates alongside other systems to gather, process, and transmit mission-critical information.
