Telemetry

The Telemetry Subsystem is essential for monitoring a satellite’s health, ensuring smooth operation, and transmitting key data to ground stations for analysis and control.

Control

The Control Subsystem ensures that the satellite maintains its correct orientation and orbit, allowing it to perform its mission effectively. It uses sensors to measure position, an onboard computer to process data, and actuators to make corrections.

Payload

The Payload Subsystem is the core of a satellite’s mission, equipped with instruments to perform data collection, communication, imaging, or scientific research. It works alongside other subsystems, such as power, control, and telemetry, to achieve mission success.

Propagation

The Propagation Subsystem ensures effective signal transmission, reception, and processing in a satellite. It consists of antennas, amplifiers, transponders, and frequency management systems to enable reliable communication between space and Earth.
